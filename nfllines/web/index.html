Each game's prediction posts once the teams' final injury reports are out: Wednesday evening for Thursday games, Friday evening for Sunday, Saturday evening for Monday. Once posted, a prediction is locked and is never changed after kickoff. The percentage is the model's chance that team wins; the letter grade shows how sure it is (A = 75%+, B+ = 65%+, B = 55%+, C+ = close to a coin flip).
